# AJ Pacific Website – AI Offering Alignment Specification

**Document Version:** 1.1  
**Date:** 3 August 2026  
**Prepared for:** AI Website Builder (Grok Build / Harness)  
**Purpose:** Complete specifications to rework the AI offering on https://dev.aj-pacific.com/ so that it is clearly structured, professionally presented, and strategically aligned with the partner site https://sas-ki-beratung.com/ (SAS Beratung GmbH). This version already includes a light partnership mention.

---

## 1. Strategic Goal

AJ Pacific and SAS Beratung have agreed to collaborate and cross-reference in the AI consulting space.

The goals of this website update are:

- Make AJ Pacific’s AI offering **clear, structured, and professional**.
- Align the **service architecture and language** with SAS so that both firms appear complementary.
- Keep and strengthen AJ Pacific’s unique strengths (Finance/Controlling depth + hands-on AI implementation + personal partnership style + Hong Kong base).
- **Already introduce a professional, modest partnership mention** with SAS Beratung and a link to their website.
- Create a clean foundation that allows easy expansion of the collaboration later.

**Important:**  
Do not overstate the relationship. Present it as a professional collaboration / partnership.  
Do not copy SAS content. Keep AJ Pacific’s own voice and positioning.

---

## 2. Overall Positioning & Tone

### Core Positioning (keep and reinforce)
- “Your Partner in Finance & AI”
- Two experienced partners (25 years Finance + 25 years Technology/AI)
- Personal partnership style: *We work with you, not for you*
- Practical solutions that deliver lasting value
- Based in Hong Kong
- Collaboration with a specialized European AI & process consulting partner

### Tone Guidelines
- Professional yet approachable
- Clear and concrete (avoid vague AI hype)
- Confident but not salesy
- Emphasize real business outcomes over technology for technology’s sake
- Short paragraphs, scannable structure
- English language throughout

### Key Messages to Amplify
- Business value first, technology second
- Practical and implementable
- Combination of financial understanding + AI capability
- Independent advice
- Knowledge transfer and enablement of the client’s team
- International collaboration (Asia + Europe)

## 6. Technical & Design Guidelines for the Builder

- Maintain the current clean, modern, minimal design language.
- Use consistent card / section styling between Controlling and AI services.
- Make the four AI services easily scannable (cards, clear headings, short bullets).
- The partnership mentions should be visually secondary (smaller text or lighter styling is fine) so they support rather than dominate.
- Keep navigation simple: Services | About | Contact.
- Ensure all internal links and the external link to https://sas-ki-beratung.com/ work correctly.
- Mobile responsiveness must remain excellent.
- Prefer a well-structured single Services page over creating many new sub-pages at this stage.
